## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the Scandinavian digital agency aesthetic: deliberate, minimal, typographic, and grounded in functional modernism. Born from Nordic design traditions and Swiss graphic discipline, the experience balances sharp intellectual precision with tactile digital craft. It targets high-end fashion, luxury lifestyle, and progressive industrial brands seeking storefronts and platforms engineered with absolute rigor.

The visual style is characterized by:
- **Architectural Framing:** Structural grid alignments, razor-thin borders, and unyielding baseline discipline.
- **Editorial Directness:** High-contrast neutral canvases, sans-serif grotesques set tight and punchy, complemented by sparse technical monospace annotations.
- **Museum-Quality Presentation:** Case studies, artifact photography, and e-commerce hero treatments are given vast negative space to breathe, allowing products and interactive showcases to command center stage without decorative clutter.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural Nordic paper, light stone, and dense black ink. Rather than pure stark `#000000` and `#FFFFFF`, it utilizes an organic cream-bone backdrop (`#F5F5F0`) paired with a deep charcoal-black (`#181818`) to recreate the presence of an archival print publication.

- **Primary (`#181818`):** The primary graphic anchor used for authoritative headlines, solid structural borders, button treatments, and crisp technical copy.
- **Secondary (`#767672`):** A muted warm gray calibrated for metadata, project indices, and subtle micro-labels.
- **Neutral (`#F5F5F0`):** The foundational canvas color that permeates the entire viewport, establishing warmth without compromising modernism.
- **Named Surfaces & Borders:** Warm stone tones (`#EAEAE3`, `#E2E2D9`) provide tactile sectioning for interactive badges, chips, and muted pill buttons without resorting to artificial elevation or drop shadows.

## Typography

Typography delivers the core graphic punch of the system. We configure an intentional pairing: **Hanken Grotesk** as the primary typographic voice and **Space Mono** as a technical counterbalance.

- **Grotesque Discipline:** All narrative statements, titles, and body content are rendered in Hanken Grotesk. Display weights are tracked tightly (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) with compressed line heights, evoking the dense, confident layout of Swiss modernist posters.
- **Monospaced Indexing:** Space Mono is used exclusively for index cataloging (e.g., `A001 NUDIE JEANS`, `A004 SAMSØE SAMSØE`), technical specifications, micro-tags, and system navigation links. All monospaced tags are set in uppercase with tracked spacing (`+0.06em` to `+0.12em`) to mirror archival inventory stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **fluid structural grid** bounded by generous outer gutters. Content breathes through ample vertical gaps, framing work with architectural deliberation.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays utilize a flexible 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Large image showcases often span full columns or sit isolated within narrow 8-column centers to accentuate vertical flow.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous section spacing (`space-xl` / 64px to 128px) separates major project showcases, giving full attention to one project before advancing to the next.
- **Mobile Behavior:** On mobile viewports, the grid consolidates to a 4-column system with tight 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Editorial image showcases retain 100% width edge-to-edge padding to maximize viewport dominance.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids skeuomorphic shadows, gradient blurs, and floating surfaces. Depth is established purely through **flat architectural layering and structural line contrast**:

- **Zero Drop-Shadows:** No component in this system casts a diffused drop shadow. The physical metaphor is printed matter, poster paper, and gallery signage.
- **Subtle Surface Containers:** Modals, pill chips, and secondary button areas distinguish themselves using tone shifts from `surface-bone` (`#F5F5F0`) to `surface-stone` (`#EAEAE3`).
- **Hairline Dividers:** Boundaries between navigation, lists, and footer segments rely on crisp 1px borders in `border-subtle` (`#DCDCD3`) or high-contrast `border-strong` (`#181818`), creating sharp spatial separations without spatial elevation.

## Shapes

The primary shape language is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**. 

- **Structural Elements:** Viewport cards, hero showcases, interactive inputs, navigation headers, and modal dialogs use strict 90-degree corners (`0px` radius). This communicates architectural permanence and engineering discipline.
- **Micro Pill Exceptions:** Dedicated secondary chips and category filter buttons may use a capsule pill geometry when explicitly contrasting against the unyielding right-angled media blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast solid `#181818` fill with crisp `#F5F5F0` text. Formed as sharp rectangles with zero radius (`roundedness: 0`), padded generously (`space-sm` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal). Hover transitions invert background and border to stone tones.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Rendered as stone pills (`surface-stone`) or transparent containers with a 1px solid border (`#181818`). Text is set in uppercase `Space Mono` or semibold `Hanken Grotesk`.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Filter chips and case study tags use compact height, uppercase monospaced labels (`Space Mono`, 11px), and a subtle stone fill (`#EAEAE3`) with no visible border. Padding is compact (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal).

### Case Study Index & Lists
- Table lists and index items span full width, divided by razor-thin `1px` lines (`border-subtle`). 
- Each list row pairs a monospaced catalog identifier (`A001`, `A002`) on the left with a bold grotesque title and right-aligned tags. Hovering a row highlights the typography or reveals an anchored media preview.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Unadorned flat text fields with a bottom border or crisp 1px perimeter box in `ink-solid`. No inner shadow. Background matches canvas `#F5F5F0`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rigid square boxes with sharp 90-degree corners. Checked state renders a solid black fill or clean geometric crosshair.

### Media Cards & Image Frames
- Showcase cards contain full-bleed project photography with no border radius. Metadata headers (`A014 SOCKSSS`) are positioned immediately above or below the asset in uppercase `Space Mono` or condensed `Hanken Grotesk`, emphasizing catalog archival precision.
